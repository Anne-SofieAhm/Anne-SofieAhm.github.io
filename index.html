Anne-Sofie Ahm is an Assistant Professor in the School of Earth and Ocean Sciences (SEOS) at the University of Victoria. She leads an interdisciplinary research group that combines geochemistry, field geology, and numerical modelling to reconstruct how Earth’s climate, oceans, and life have co-evolved. (Read more about ongoing research projects...).

Ahm received the 2026 CUFA Early in Career Award in recognition of her research, teaching, and mentorship.

Ahm's research group is part of a vibrant Earth history node at SEOS, which also includes students and postdocs that work with Jon Husson and Blake Dyer.
